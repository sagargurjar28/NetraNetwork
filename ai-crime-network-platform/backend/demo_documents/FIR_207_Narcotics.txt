FIRST INFORMATION REPORT
FIR No: 207/2025
Police Station: Kochi Port, Kerala
Date: 18 September 2025

Complainant: Deputy Commissioner of Customs (Intelligence).

Facts: Acting on specific intelligence, a joint operation was conducted at
Kochi Port on 17 September 2025. Three containers declared as "marine
equipment" were intercepted. On examination, 47 kg of contraband was recovered
concealed in the container walls.

Documents recovered indicate shipment was arranged through Rahul Verma,
a local clearing agent operating since 2019. His phone +91-98xxxx5555 was in
constant contact with Mumbai-based numbers during the two weeks preceding
the shipment.

Informant Deepak Joshi, a port worker, had earlier reported unusual activity
at the yard on multiple occasions in August 2025. He identified Rahul Verma
as the person supervising late-night loading operations.

Financial records show Rs 25,00,000 transferred from HDFC A/c 1234 to
SBI A/c 5678 on 8 September 2025, believed to be payment for the shipment.
The SBI account is held by a company directed by Anita Desai, operating from
a Delhi Office.

Photographs and seized documents attached.
