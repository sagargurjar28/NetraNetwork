FIRST INFORMATION REPORT
FIR No: 312/2025
Police Station: Cyber Cell, Gurgaon
Date: 22 September 2025

Complainant: Neha Gupta, resident of Pune.

Facts: Between 15 August and 10 September 2025, the complainant received calls
from +91-97xxxx2222. Callers posed as officials from a foreign embassy, claiming
the complainant's son was in legal trouble abroad and demanding immediate
payment. The complainant transferred Rs 8,00,000 in four instalments to
bank accounts provided by the callers.

Technical analysis traces +91-97xxxx2222 to a call center operating from
Gurgaon Center. The center is registered to Priya Sharma. Call records show
47 simultaneous lines and outbound volume exceeding 12,000 calls per day.

Further analysis shows +91-97xxxx2222 placed repeated calls to +91-99xxxx4444
(a UAE number) during the same period, believed to be operational coordination.

Investigation revealed Priya Sharma was seen meeting Suresh Nair at the
Gurgaon Center on 5 September 2025. Both are known associates of Vikram Singh.

Neha Gupta's funds were transferred through ICICI A/c 9012 into a chain of
accounts, ultimately routing to HDFC A/c 1234. The pattern is consistent with
layered money laundering.

Cyber Cell requests assistance from EOW for financial trail analysis.
