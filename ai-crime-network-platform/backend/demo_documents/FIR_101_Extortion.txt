FIRST INFORMATION REPORT
FIR No: 101/2025
Police Station: Andheri, Mumbai
Date: 12 September 2025

Complainant: Ravi Kumar, resident of Andheri East, Mumbai.

Facts: On the night of 11 September 2025, the complainant received repeated
threatening phone calls from +91-98xxxx5555. The caller identified himself as
Suresh Nair and demanded Rs 5,00,000, threatening harm to the complainant's
family. The complainant had previously refused to pay protection money for
his transport business.

During investigation, call detail records show 47 calls between the complainant
and +91-98xxxx5555 over the last 60 days, all between 22:00 and 02:00 hours.
The number +91-98xxxx5555 was registered under Suresh Nair, resident of
Mumbai Warehouse premises, Malad.

Further enquiries reveal Suresh Nair operates under instructions from
Vikram Singh, who controls a narcotics distribution network in the
western suburbs. Vikram Singh is known to travel to Dubai frequently.

The complainant's family has been relocated for safety. Investigation ongoing.
